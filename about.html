About the Company

Mercovyn LLC — Manufacturer & Importer of Premium Baler Twine

Mercovyn LLC supplies heavy-duty polypropylene baler twine in Armenia. The twine is produced in Iran and imported for farms, distributors, and agricultural businesses. With 20 years of manufacturing know-how, we deliver export-ready packaging and regional supply with custom colors made to order.
